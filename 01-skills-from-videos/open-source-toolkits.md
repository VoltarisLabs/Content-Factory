# Open source toolkits we studied

Public repos we found in creators' videos and studied.

| Toolkit | By | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| [herk-edit-kit](https://github.com/nateherkai/herk-edit-kit) | Nate Herk | A playbook for AI assisted video editing |
| [lemo-opuscar](https://github.com/lemomo-ai/lemo-opuscar) | Lemomo (@lemomo_ai) | 59 visual film styles plus notes on directing |
| [OpenMontage](https://github.com/calesthio/OpenMontage) | OpenMontage | Agent skills and components for video production |
| [youtube-agent-skill](https://github.com/Jakeschincariol/youtube-agent-skill) | Jake Schincariol | Skills for scripting, packaging and niche research |
| [whiteboard-animation-vi](https://github.com/letuandat131/whiteboard-animation-vi) | letuandat131 | Turns a picture into a hand drawn video |
| [gods-eye-view](https://github.com/bilawalsidhu/gods-eye-view) | Bilawal Sidhu | A live 3D globe of public flight, ship and satellite feeds |

**One lesson from the YouTube toolkit:** judge a video against its own channel's median, not against raw views.
