# The first second

**The opener caps everything after it.**
On 2026-09-01 we cut the same project two ways. With a 35% skip rate it reached 3,032. With a 54% skip rate it reached 1,468, even though its saves and comments per 1k were double.

**A skip rate above 50% means the reel won't break out.**
0 of 65 posts with a skip rate above 50% ever passed 10k reach.

**Open on real footage of a physical thing.**
In the last 20 posts up to 2026-10-24, openers made of real footage had a median skip of 47%. Drawn graphics, cartoons or UI cards on frame one skipped 60 to 79% (7 of 7).

**Don't open on the presenter's face.**
5 of 5 posts that showed the presenter's face in the first second skipped 55 to 73%.

**The spectacle has to be real and move at real speed.**
On 2026-10-27 a slowed down doll shot was rejected. Real footage of a sinking yacht won.
