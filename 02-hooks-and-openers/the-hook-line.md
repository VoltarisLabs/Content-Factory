# The hook line

**Say it flat. Never open with "if".**
Openers that started with a condition ("If you're paying for AI video...") had a median reach of 625, and 0 of 11 passed 10k. All 7 of our posts over 10k opened with a flat statement.

**Land the tension word early.**
6 of those 7 posts put the tension word inside the first three words.

**Name an institution.**
Hooks naming an institution (a government, a bank, the military, a big company) had a median reach of 2,554, which is 1.9 times the rest (n=53, measured 2026-08-28). An earlier note claimed 3.3x. That figure was inflated by two 100k outliers, and we corrected it.

**A hook with no subject costs viewers.**
First lines that didn't say what the reel was about skipped around 55%.

**Statements, not questions.**
We retired question style hooks on 2026-10-23 and now use flat statements only.
