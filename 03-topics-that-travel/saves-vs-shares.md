# Saves vs shares

**A free tool is a save. Something done to you is a share.**
People send a reel when it's about them and they can picture who to send it to. So the question to ask isn't "is this a good tool" but "who did what to the viewer".

**Shares are the widest gap between hits and the rest.**
Our top 5 posts averaged 27.1 shares per 1k reached. Everything else averaged 6.8.

**Maxing saves and comments isn't enough on its own.**
One post had our best ever saves (59.3 per 1k) and comments (64.0 per 1k) but only 13.6 shares per 1k, and it reached 1,468.

**Comments don't separate the giants.**
Our two 100k posts had 17.1 comments per 1k. Everything since had 16.6, almost the same. What differed was saves (50.5 vs 21.4) and shares (24.3 vs 8.6).
