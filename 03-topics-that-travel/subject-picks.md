# Subject picks

**The one shape that wins: an institution, big money, and somebody gave it away.**
Our top 15 posts ever all follow it. A named company charges a lot for a physical thing, then someone releases it for free.

**AI tool subjects don't travel.**
Across 156 posts, AI tool subjects had a median reach of 665, and 0 ever passed 10k. Physical things with no AI word had a median of 2,178, and 8 passed 10k.

**The viewer has to be able to take it home.**
Posts offering something the viewer can actually get (a free repo, an open source tool, a download) had a median of 1,629, and 7 of 76 passed 10k. Posts where the viewer couldn't have it had a median of 672, and 0 of 15 passed 10k.

**Small prices make small reels.**
"X charges $15 a month, here's a free one" hooks had a median of 828 (n=17). Hooks naming an institution, government or military had a median of 2,900 (n=15).

**Make sure the viewer can actually run it.**
A tool that needs a high end GPU got 0 saves. Most viewers can't run it, so nobody kept it.
