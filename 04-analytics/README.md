# Analytics

What our own Instagram numbers proved between August and October 2026.
Reach on a single post is noisy, so these signals tell you more.

| File | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [watch-time.md](watch-time.md) | Completion, seconds watched, and the "shorter is better" trap |
| [hashtags.md](hashtags.md) | Which hashtags sat above and below median reach |

## Signals at a glance

| Signal | What we measured | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| Skip rate | 0 of 65 posts above 50% skip passed 10k | The opener is the gate |
| Completion | 65 to 80% completion had a median reach of 19,716 | Watch time drives reach |
| Save rate | Predicts reach (r = +0.371) even on small posts | Read it before reach |
| Shares per 1k | Top 5 posts 27.1 vs 6.8 for the rest | The widest gap |
| Reach alone | The same reel posted twice did 568 and 11,192 | One post's reach is mostly luck |
