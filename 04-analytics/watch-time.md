# Watch time

**Completion is the lever.**
Measured on 62 posts on 2026-09-02 (median length 19s, median completion 45%):

| Completion | Posts | Median reach |
|---|---|---|
| Under 50% | 40 | 1,412 |
| 50 to 65% | 14 | 2,954 |
| 65 to 80% | 5 | 19,716 |

**Seconds watched matter too.**
Median reach by seconds watched: under 7s got 619, 7 to 9s got 1,572, 9 to 11s got 1,876, and 11 to 14s got 4,460.

**Shorter isn't automatically better.**
Reels of 12 to 16 seconds had the best completion (59%) but the worst median reach (1,236). A short reel finishes often but gives too few seconds of watch time.
