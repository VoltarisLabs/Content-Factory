# The October slump (17 to 24 Oct 2026)

**What happened:** the last 10 posts had a median reach of 534 and a 64.2% skip rate. The account medians are 1,640 reach and 49% skip.

**What we found (156 posts):**
- 6 of those 10 posts were about AI tools. AI tool subjects have never passed 10k on this account.
- An AI tool has no physical picture, so those openers were drawn graphics, and drawn openers skipped 60 to 79%.
- Posts with the presenter's face in the first second skipped 55 to 73% (5 of 5).

**Lesson:** the three problems travel together. Pick a subject with a real, physical thing to show, and the opener problem mostly solves itself.
