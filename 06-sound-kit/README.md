# Sound kit

Free sound effects to use in reels, plus the background music picks we like.

| File | What it covers |
|---|---|
| [sfx/](sfx/) | Free sound effects you can use anywhere (CC0) |
| [more-free-sfx.md](more-free-sfx.md) | More effects to download from Mixkit |
| [background-music.md](background-music.md) | Music picks and what we learned about music on reels |

## Included sound effects

| File | Sound | Good for |
|---|---|---|
| [sfx/pop_deep.wav](sfx/pop_deep.wav) | A deep pop | A word or card appearing |
| [sfx/pin_drop.wav](sfx/pin_drop.wav) | A light pin drop | A small reveal or a number landing |
| [sfx/tick.wav](sfx/tick.wav) | A short tick | A counter or a list item |

They're all by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) and released as CC0. See [sfx/LICENSE.txt](sfx/LICENSE.txt).

**One lesson:** use sound effects sparingly. A sound should match something that happens on screen.
