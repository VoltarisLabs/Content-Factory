## What we learned about music

**Add the sound in the Instagram app, don't bake it into the video.**
Instagram only credits a reel to a trending sound when you attach it from its audio library at posting time. Then the reel shows on that sound's page. A song mixed into the video file counts as "original audio" and gets no boost.

**Commercial music baked into the file can mute the whole reel.**
Platforms fingerprint label recordings. A match can mute the entire audio track, voice included.

**Pick instrumentals under a voice.**
Vocals mask the speaker's consonants. On one reel a vocal track made "the repo" sound like "the review".

**Check what the account can use.**
Business accounts only get Meta's royalty free Sound Collection. Creator accounts get the full library.
