# More free sound effects

These are free to use in videos, but Mixkit's licence doesn't allow sharing the files in a repo like this one. So download them yourself from [Mixkit's free sound effects](https://mixkit.co/free-sound-effects/) by searching the word in the first column.

| Search for | Good for |
|---|---|
| whoosh | A change to a different kind of shot |
| impact | A hit on a big word or a crash on screen |
| riser | Building up to a reveal |
| ding | A keyword or a correct answer |
| typing | Someone typing on a laptop or phone on screen |
| camera shutter | A screenshot or photo moment |

**Avoid** pulling "no copyright" sound effects off YouTube. Many have spoken intros, and their real licence is often unclear.
