# Audio mix for reels

## When to use
Once picture and captions are locked.

## Steps
1. Clean the voice: a high pass filter around 80 Hz, gentle noise reduction and light compression.
2. Lay the music under the voice, 15 to 25 dB quieter than the voice while someone is talking.
3. Duck the music a little further under the most important lines.
4. Add sound effects only where something happens on screen, and land each one on the exact frame.
5. Bring the whole mix to about -14 LUFS integrated, with the true peak below -1 dBTP.

## Rules
- The voice is always the loudest thing.
- Use instrumental music under a voice. Vocals hide the speaker's words.
- Use sound effects sparingly. One sound per on-screen event, never a sound on every cut.
- Only use music and sounds you have the rights to. Add trending songs inside the app when posting, not in the file.

## Check before moving on
- Listen on phone speakers and on earbuds.
- Every word is clear over the music.
- Nothing clips or distorts at the loudest moment.
