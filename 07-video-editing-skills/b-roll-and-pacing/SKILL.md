# B-roll and pacing

## When to use
Once the voice track is tight, to cover jump cuts and show the story.

## Steps
1. Go line by line through the script and write down the one thing each line should show.
2. Find footage of that exact thing. Use your own footage, licensed stock, or clips you have the right to use.
3. Place each clip so it lands on the word it illustrates, not before it.
4. Change what is on screen every 1 to 3 seconds. Go faster through lists and hold longer on the reveal.
5. Use a straight cut by default. Save transitions for moments when the picture changes to a different kind of shot.

## Rules
- The first frame must move and show the subject. No logo, no intro card, no black frame.
- Real moving footage beats stills and screenshots. If a still is unavoidable, give it slow movement.
- Don't use the same clip twice in one video.
- B-roll fills the frame. Avoid small boxed clips floating on a background.
- Blur or crop out anything you don't want on screen: names, logos, private details.

## Check before moving on
- No stretch longer than about 3s without a change on screen.
- Every clip matches the words under it.
- Every clip can be traced to footage you're allowed to use.
