# Cut the dead air

## When to use
On any talking head or voiceover take, before b-roll and captions go on.

## Steps
1. Watch the whole take once without cutting. Mark the best read of each line.
2. Remove repeated takes. Keep the cleanest read, or the one with the most energy.
3. Cut silences longer than about 0.3s between sentences. Leave a short breath of 0.1 to 0.2s so the speech doesn't sound glued together.
4. Trim false starts ("So the, the thing is...") back to the clean start of the word.
5. Cut on the waveform, not only the transcript. Transcripts hide half words and breaths.
6. Play it back at normal speed and fix any cut that clips the first sound or the tail of a word.

## Rules
- Never cut inside a word.
- Don't speed up the voice to save time. Cut the pauses instead.
- Hide jump cuts with b-roll or a small punch in on the speaker (about 110 to 120%).
- Don't end on a frozen frame. End on movement or on the last word.

## Check before moving on
- No silence over about 0.3s is left.
- Every line is heard once.
- Lip sync still holds after every cut.
