# Export for social

## When to use
When the edit and the mix are final.

## Settings
| Setting | Value |
|---|---|
| Size | 1080x1920 (9:16) |
| Frame rate | Same as the source footage, usually 30 fps |
| Video | H.264, high quality, about 15 to 20 Mbps |
| Audio | AAC, 48 kHz, 320 kbps |

## Rules
- Pick one frame rate for the project at the start. Converting between 24, 25 and 30 fps mid edit causes stutter and drifting sync.
- Use even numbers for width and height, or the picture can shift or stretch.
- Keep a named master file of every final export.

## Check before posting
- Watch the full export once, start to finish, with sound.
- The first frame is strong and moving.
- Audio and lips stay in sync to the last second.
- Captions are inside the safe area and spelled right.
- The cover frame reads clearly as a small thumbnail.
