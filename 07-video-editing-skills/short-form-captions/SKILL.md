# Short-form captions

## When to use
After the picture is locked, so the caption timing doesn't move again.

## Steps
1. Get word-level timing from a transcript, then correct every name, number and brand against the script.
2. Split the text into chunks of 1 to 4 words, breaking at natural pauses.
3. Make each chunk appear on its first word's start and leave on the next chunk's start.
4. Give one keyword per chunk an accent colour or a larger size.
5. Place the captions inside the safe area (see the rules below).

## Rules
- Use a heavy sans serif font, large enough to read on a phone at arm's length (about 60 to 90px on a 1080px wide frame).
- Give the text an outline or shadow so it reads over any footage.
- Safe area on a 1080x1920 frame: keep text out of the top 250px, the bottom 400px and the right 150px. The platform's caption, username and buttons sit there.
- Never cover the speaker's face or the key action.
- Keep the style the same through the whole video, and change only the accent colour between videos.

## Check before moving on
- Every caption is spelled right, including the numbers.
- No caption sits under the app's buttons when you preview it on a phone.
- The captions never drift ahead of or behind the voice.
